How it works

Three steps from curiosity to confident fingerspelling.

  1. 1

    Show a sign or enter text

    Hold an ISL alphabet sign in the camera box, or type (or speak) any English or Kannada text.

  2. 2

    AI processes the input

    The convolutional neural network classifies your handshape locally, or the translator maps your words letter by letter.

  3. 3

    Learn, translate, and practice

    Watch finger-spelling sequences build up, check your accuracy, and repeat until the signs feel natural.
